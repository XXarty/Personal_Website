Working projects

Work in progress· Personal project· Supervisor: Prof. Paul Whelan

The Price of Duration Risk in Emerging Markets

A security-level panel of emerging-market sovereign bonds from LSEG (1995–2025) and maturity-weighted debt-to-GDP measures of duration supply, used to extend the Greenwood–Vayanos (2014) framework to settings where inflation and exchange-rate exposure change the pricing problem.

GitHub· Report· Data· Slides

Work in progress· With Prof. Paul Whelan, Dr. Niklas Kroner, Ms. Linda Lee Zhang

Cryptocurrency Overnight Drift

Five-minute spot data from Binance, Coinbase and OKX document a robust concentration of positive BTC and ETH returns in the 5:00–6:00 p.m. ET hour, with Newey–West-adjusted inference and cross-exchange robustness tests.

Completed· Apr 2025· CUHK industrial project · Supervisor: Prof. Paul Whelan & Mr. Jason Ng

Crypto Spot–Perpetual Futures Arbitrage on Binance

A mean-reversion signal engine on hourly spot–perp spreads (2020–2025), a Python backtester with position management and capital constraints, and an extension to funding-rate modelling in the cash-and-carry leg.
